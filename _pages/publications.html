My research focuses on fault creep: why some faults slip slowly and steadily while others lock up and rupture in earthquakes. I look at the geomorphic and mineral controls on fault behaviour, and what they mean for seismic hazard. My earlier work at the University of Auckland investigated the Mangatangi Fault in the Hunua Range and Auckland's historic seismicity.

See also my research posters and talks.
