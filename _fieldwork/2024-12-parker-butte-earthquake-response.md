On December 9, 2024 a magnitude 5.7 earthquake struck near Parker Butte, Nevada. I joined the Nevada Seismological Laboratory's field response team, deploying temporary seismic stations to record aftershocks and checking the area for ground deformation.

We documented the response in a short video series:
